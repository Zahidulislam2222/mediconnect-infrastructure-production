# Code of conduct

MediConnect adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct.

In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best
for patients and the community, and never harass anyone. Health topics can be personal, so never
share anyone's health information, including your own, in issues or discussions.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the contact options on the
maintainer's GitHub profile (https://github.com/Zahidulislam2222). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove
content or block participants who break these rules.
